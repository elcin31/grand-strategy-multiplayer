# DOMINION — National Course

Forty original programs extend the existing modern-world campaign. Four generic branches contain five nodes each; five regional programs and three-node chains for USA, Russia, China, Germany and UK provide distinct economic and administrative choices. `focusCatalogue.ts` is the authoritative graph, costs, durations, rewards and eligibility source. No reference-game tree or external political ideology is imported.

Only one course runs per country. Starting pays the fixed quoted treasury and political cost once. Actual monthly ticks advance dated progress; default pauses without accumulating missed work. Cancellation clears the course without refund. Exclusive alternatives permanently lock their sibling and either alternative permits a shared successor. Scientific prerequisites use actual owned named discoveries.

Completion appends the unique dated ledger and removes the project before granting cash, reserve, stability, political power, reputation, existing army experience or peaceful neighbor relations. Tax, production/resources, trade, administration, military upkeep, research, construction, supply, combat, manpower growth and diplomatic acceptance derive bounded permanent effects from that ledger. There is no duplicate persistent bonus balance. Replays and save/restores cannot pay a course twice.

Schema18 adds `completedFocuses`, optional `nationalFocus`, and bounded `focusEvents`. Strict current saves reject wrong-country programs, unknown IDs/fields, missing prerequisites, impossible progress or dates, simultaneous exclusive choices and repeated/orphan completion events. Existing schema17 saves keep exact pre-migration backup bytes. Authenticated commands retain the existing actor, CAS and receipt infrastructure.

The landscape panel mounts only while selected. It provides branch/region/major tabs, a scrollable graph, predecessor navigation, real progress/costs/rewards and reasons, plus cancellation and dated history. AI chooses affordable eligible programs in the existing scheduled strategic slots, preserving reserves. Monthly evaluation, bounded WeakMap bonus selectors and event-driven history perform no work on camera frames.
