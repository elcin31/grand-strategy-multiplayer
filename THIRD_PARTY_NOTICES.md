# Geographic data

Map contours derive from Natural Earth, 110m admin_0_countries, public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
The exact source SHA256 is in `src/map/source.json`. Rebuild with `scripts/import-map.py`.

Natural Earth depicts generalized geographic boundaries, including disputed territories; it is not an authoritative statement of sovereignty. The current European scenario's provinces are generated by geographic cuts. They are not administrative subdivisions.

Terrain bands, rivers, lakes and city catalogue are original stylized scenario data. Elevation shading is visual, not real-world DEM data. No Age of History code, images, maps, UI or audio assets are included.

## Country flag vectors

flag-icons 7.5.0 by Panayiotis Lipiridis, MIT licence. Original source: https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons . The complete licence is in `src/world/data/flag-icons-LICENSE.txt` and embedded in `src/world/data/source.json` / `worldDataNotices`. The country catalogue references these offline vectors by ISO code. No flag assets from another game are used.

Country adjectives and the deterministic neighbor-aware color assignment are authored in this repository. The catalogue retains Natural Earth's source population year and does not claim a current census.
